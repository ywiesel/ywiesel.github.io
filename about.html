About Me

I’m a Computer Science student at Clemson University who enjoys building things, solving problems, and learning how technology works behind the scenes.

My background is primarily in software development, with experience in Java, C++, Python, JavaScript, React, and Flask. I’ve worked on projects involving full-stack development, algorithms, data structures, APIs, databases, and web applications. One of my main projects is a full-stack mini search engine featuring web crawling, inverted indexing, ranked search results, image search, and analytics. I’ve also worked on algorithm-focused projects that have given me experience applying data structures and problem-solving techniques to real applications. Recently, I’ve been expanding my interests into IT, networking, and infrastructure. I’m interested in learning how systems communicate, how networks are designed and maintained, and how technical problems can be diagnosed and solved.

I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, build, and gain hands-on experience. Whether I’m developing software, troubleshooting a system, or learning a new technology, I enjoy understanding how things work and finding ways to make them better.
